Feature overview

The same client covers everyday privacy and the stricter networks where a plain tunnel is not enough.

Smart routing

Send only the apps you choose through FalconX. Local printers, banks, and home devices can stay on the direct path.

Kill switch / App Guard

If the tunnel drops, App Guard pauses protected apps until the connection returns, so traffic does not leak onto the open network.

VPN sharing

Share one household connection with the five devices on a personal plan, or place the applet on a router for everything on that Wi-Fi.

Site and app filter

Block or allow lists live in the app. Useful on a family router when you want the tunnel on, but not every destination.

Port forwarding

Selected servers can open an inbound port for a client that needs it. Turn it on per device from the member area.
