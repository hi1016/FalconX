When to leave WireGuard

March 12, 2026 · Protocols

WireGuard is the right default. It reconnects quickly and uses the dedicated servers well. Some networks still refuse it because the handshake does not look like everyday browsing.

If the app spins and never reaches “protected,” switch to OpenWeb first. If that also fails, Shadow Mode is the option built for those networks. Turn App Guard on before you experiment so a failed tunnel does not dump traffic into the open.
