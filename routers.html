FalconX on a router

Put the tunnel on the router and phones, consoles, and TVs on that Wi-Fi can use it without their own app.

The applet is offered for a short list of compatible models. Install only the build named for that hardware. After it is on, pick a city, enable App Guard, and optionally turn on site filters for the whole LAN.

This demo does not include firmware images.
