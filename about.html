About FalconX

FalconX is a demo storefront for a VPN aimed at people who work across borders and networks that interfere with ordinary tunnels.

The product story starts in 2012: dedicated servers instead of crowded virtual machines, a protocol option called Shadow Mode, and a support desk that stays open overnight.

Personal plans cover a household. Business plans cover seats. VIP adds a dedicated address and a shorter queue. None of the checkout on this site charges a card.

FalconX is not affiliated with Astrill or any other VPN brand. Names, reviews, and prices here were written for this demo.
