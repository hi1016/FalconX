Dedicated IPs

A dedicated address stays yours. Remote desktops and allow-listed services stop seeing a shared exit. VIP includes one. Other plans can add it.

Multi-hop

Multi-hop enters in one country and exits in another. Use it when you want the entry and exit separated. It costs some speed, which the app shows before you connect.
