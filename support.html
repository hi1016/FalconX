FAQ

A personal plan allows five devices online at the same time. VIP raises that to seven. Business is billed per seat.
Shadow Mode is FalconX’s protocol option for networks that interfere with ordinary VPN traffic. Turn it on in the app when a standard protocol will not connect.
The published policy says FalconX does not keep a record of sites visited or traffic contents. Account and payment records are kept for billing. Read the privacy page for the demo wording.
The demo refund window is 7 days on first-time personal plans if the apps were not a fit. Details are on the refund page.
