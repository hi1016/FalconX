Privacy policy

Demo wording. This site does not operate a VPN service.

Account email, plan choice, and password for the demo stay in local storage on your device. Contact form notes stay there too.

The IP tool calls a public lookup service only when you press the button. FalconX does not receive that result on a server of its own.

A production version of this product would not keep records of sites you visit or the contents of your traffic. Billing records would be kept to run the subscription.
